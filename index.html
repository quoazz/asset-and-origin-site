From brief to introduction, in four steps.

  1. Step 1

    Tell us what you need

    We agree the brief with you: what is required, what is preferred, budget, location and timing.

  2. Step 2

    We search and verify

    We go to our network of dealers, collectors and private owners and to the open market, then call each seller to confirm the asset exists and is available.

  3. Step 3

    Pay only for a result

    We tell you how many verified assets we found and the fee. You decide whether to go ahead; only then do you pay.

  4. Step 4

    Meet the seller

    You receive the list with our notes, and we put you in direct contact with the seller.

Your requirement comes first
We have no stock to move. The search is shaped by your brief, and a candidate that doesn't meet a required point is not presented as a match.
Hours back in your week
Instead of checking listings, forums and dealers every day, you receive a short list of assets that match your brief and are confirmed available.
Verified, not just listed
Before an asset goes on your list, we contact the seller ourselves to confirm it exists and is available.
A network, not just a search
The right asset is often with a dealer we know or a private owner who has not listed it yet. We reach them directly, so you see options that never make it to public listings.
Extra services when you need them
Independent inspection, shipping and transport documents can be added to any search and are quoted separately.
