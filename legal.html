Terms & Conditions

These terms apply to every sourcing engagement with Asset & Origin, for private clients and for businesses. You accept them at checkout, just before payment.

  1. Scope

    These terms apply to all services of Asset & Origin (details in the Imprint below) for clients who are consumers and clients who act for a business. Different terms of the client do not apply unless we agree to them in writing.

  2. The service

    We search for assets that match the brief you give us, contact the seller of each one directly to confirm that the asset exists and is available, and introduce you to the seller. We are not a party to any purchase of an asset: the purchase contract is concluded directly between you and the seller.

    Additional services are not part of the sourcing service; see the next clause.

  3. Additional services

    On request we can arrange independent inspection of an asset, shipping, and transport and export documents. These are separate services: we quote each one in writing before it starts, and it is paid separately from the sourcing fee.

    Where they are carried out by third parties, such as inspectors, shipping companies or customs agents, those parties' own terms also apply. We choose and coordinate them with care, but we are not responsible for how they perform their work unless we have agreed otherwise in writing.

  4. The brief and the search

    Before we start, we agree the brief with you in writing (email is enough): what is required, what is preferred, budget, location and timing. How long a search takes depends on the type of asset; we give you an indication at the start, but no fixed deadline unless agreed in writing.

  5. No fee until we find a match

    You pay nothing until we have found something. When we have found at least one asset that matches your brief and whose existence we have verified, we tell you how many we have found and the fee. If you want to proceed, you pay the sourcing fee in full and in advance; we then send you the list with the details of each asset.

    If we find no matching asset, no fee is due.

    The sourcing fee pays for the search and for the list of verified assets, not for a purchase. It is not refunded if you decide not to buy any of the assets on the list.

  6. If a listed asset is no longer available

    Before we deliver the list, we contact the seller of each asset directly, usually by phone, to confirm that it exists and is available. Good assets can still sell quickly. If an asset on the list turns out to be no longer available, tell us within 48 hours of receiving the list. Once we have checked it with the seller, we refund the part of the fee for that asset: the fee divided by the number of assets on the list. For example, if the list has four assets for a fee of USD 1,000 and one is no longer available, we refund USD 250. If none of the assets is available, we refund the full fee.

    No refund is due for an asset reported after 48 hours, or for an asset you decide not to buy.

  7. Ongoing search and other fees

    If you want us to keep the search open and send you new matches as they appear, we agree this with you individually in writing. For particularly rare or high-value assets, a different fee structure, such as a commission or a fee on completion of the purchase, may apply; it is always agreed in writing before the search starts, including when it becomes due.

    All prices are in US dollars (USD). If you withdraw before we send the list, you pay an amount in proportion to the service already provided.
